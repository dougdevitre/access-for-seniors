# Access For Seniors — Community Features

## Capabilities

- Interest-based group matching (AI-suggested based on resident profiles)
- Activity calendar (facility-managed)
- Group conversation facilitation (moderated by AI or caregiver)
- Photo sharing (with per-photo consent)
- Virtual events (for remote family participation)

## Privacy

- All group interactions are opt-in
- Residents can leave any group at any time
- No cross-group data leakage
- Photos require explicit consent per photo; residents can request removal

## Staff Tools

- Activity scheduling with resident interest matching
- Attendance tracking (manual or badge-based)
- Group engagement reports (participation rates, interest trends)
