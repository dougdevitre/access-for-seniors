# Access For Seniors — Go-to-Market

## Positioning

**One-liner:** Access For Seniors helps senior living communities detect isolation early and
keep families connected — powered by AI, guided by caregivers.

**Tagline options:**
- "Every senior deserves to feel connected."
- "AI-powered connection for the people who matter most."

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Segment | Size | Decision Maker | Champion |
|---------|------|----------------|----------|
| Independent living communities | 50–200 residents | Executive Director | Activities Director |
| Assisted living facilities | 20–100 residents | Administrator | Director of Nursing |
| Continuing care retirement communities (CCRC) | 200+ residents | VP of Operations | Resident Services Director |
| Home health agencies | 100+ clients | Owner/CEO | Care Coordinator |
| Health systems (enterprise) | 500+ beds across facilities | VP of Senior Services | Innovation Lead |

## Messaging Framework

| Audience | Pain Point | Message |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Executive Director | Resident satisfaction scores, family complaints | "Proactive connection reduces complaints and improves satisfaction scores" |
| Activities Director | Can't reach every resident; activity attendance declining | "AI companions fill the gaps between activities and ensure no one is forgotten" |
| Family Member | Guilt about not visiting enough; worry about parent | "Stay connected to what matters in Mom's day — without the guilt" |
| Director of Nursing | Staff burnout; missed behavioral changes | "Early alerts on engagement changes so your team can intervene before crisis" |

## Brand Voice

- Calm, professional, empathetic
- Practical over poetic
- No fear-based messaging about loneliness or aging
- No hype about AI — focus on outcomes for residents and families
- Imagery: warm, diverse, real (no stock photo seniors; no robots)

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Strength | Our Differentiation |
|-----------|----------|---------------------|
| Generic telehealth | Clinical focus | We focus on connection, not clinical care |
| Social robots (e.g., ElliQ) | Hardware presence | We're software-first; lower cost, easier deployment |
| Activity management software | Scheduling | We add AI-powered 1:1 engagement between activities |
| Family communication apps | Family-to-facility messaging | We proactively generate connection, not just facilitate it |
