# Access For Seniors — Aging Services Navigation

## Philosophy

Access For Seniors connects older adults and their families to aging-services programs
with the same trauma-informed, community-grounded approach as other "Access To" tools.
The goal: reduce friction between a need and the program that meets it.

**Language rules:**
- Person-first: "older adult" or "senior", not "elderly" (unless the program uses that term)
- Plain language over bureaucratic jargon ("help with meals" vs "Title III-C nutrition services")
- Respect autonomy — offer options, never prescribe
- Acknowledge barriers (transportation, language, digital literacy) and plan around them

## Primary Programs

### Older Americans Act (OAA) — acl.gov

| Title | Program | Purpose | Access Point |
|-------|---------|---------|--------------|
| III-B | Supportive Services | Transportation, legal aid, in-home services, adult day care | Local Area Agency on Aging (AAA) |
| III-C1 | Congregate Meals | Free meals at senior centers | Local senior centers |
| III-C2 | Home-Delivered Meals | Meals on Wheels for homebound seniors | Meals on Wheels / AAA |
| III-D | Health Promotion | Evidence-based wellness and disease prevention | AAA, senior centers |
| III-E | Family Caregiver Support | Respite, counseling, training for caregivers | AAA |
| VII | Elder Rights | Long-Term Care Ombudsman, legal assistance, elder abuse prevention | State Ombudsman, AAA |

### Medicare — medicare.gov

| Part | Coverage | Cost (2025 est.) |
|------|----------|------------------|
| Part A | Hospital, skilled nursing (short-term), hospice, some home health | Usually premium-free |
| Part B | Doctor visits, outpatient, preventive, DME | ~$185/month (income-adjusted) |
| Part C | Medicare Advantage (alternative to A+B, often includes dental/vision/Rx) | Varies |
| Part D | Prescription drugs | Varies |
| Medigap | Supplement to fill Original Medicare gaps | Varies |

**Enrollment periods:**
- **Initial:** 3 months before to 3 months after turning 65
- **General:** Jan 1 – Mar 31 (late enrollment in Part B; penalty may apply)
- **Open (AEP):** Oct 15 – Dec 7 (switch Advantage or Part D)
- **Special:** After qualifying event (job loss, move, etc.)

**Free counseling:** SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program) — shiphelp.org

### Medicaid — medicaid.gov

- **LTSS (Long-Term Services and Supports):** Home and community-based services (HCBS) waivers, nursing facility care
- **Dual eligibility:** Seniors on both Medicare and Medicaid get additional support
- **Eligibility:** State-specific; generally income + asset limits
- **Apply:** State Medicaid agency or local DSS office

## Support Networks

### Area Agencies on Aging (AAA)
- Local hubs for aging services
- **Find your AAA:** Eldercare Locator — 1-800-677-1116 — eldercare.acl.gov
- AAAs coordinate OAA programs, caregiver support, benefits counseling

### Aging and Disability Resource Centers (ADRC)
- Single entry point for aging AND disability services
- No wrong door — can screen for multiple programs at once
- Access via 211 or local AAA

### Long-Term Care Ombudsman
- Advocate for residents in nursing homes and assisted living
- State-level program under OAA Title VII
- Handles complaints about care, rights violations

### Caregiver Support

| Resource | What It Offers |
|---------|----------------|
| OAA Title III-E | Respite care, counseling, training, support groups |
| Family Caregiver Alliance (caregiver.org) | Online resources, state-by-state program directory |
| AARP Caregiver Resource Center | Guides, checklists, legal planning info |
| ARCH National Respite Network | Find respite care providers |

## Specialty Resources

### Food & Nutrition
- **SNAP for seniors:** Simplified application in many states
- **Senior Farmers' Market Nutrition Program:** Coupons for low-income seniors
- **Commodity Supplemental Food Program (CSFP):** Monthly food package for 60+ at income limits
- **Meals on Wheels:** mealsonwheelsamerica.org

### Housing
- **HUD Section 202:** Affordable housing for very low-income seniors
- **Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP):** Utility bill help
- **Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP):** Home energy efficiency

### Prescription Assistance
- **Medicare Extra Help:** Low-income subsidy for Part D
- **State Pharmaceutical Assistance Programs (SPAP):** State-level Rx help
- **Patient Assistance Programs (PAP):** Manufacturer-sponsored free/low-cost meds

### Transportation
- OAA Title III-B transportation services via AAA
- Medicaid Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT)
- GoGoGrandparent, Uber/Lyft senior programs
- Local volunteer driver programs

## Integration Pattern

When a resident or family member expresses a need, the Access For Seniors assistant should:

1. **Listen & acknowledge** — Never rush to solutions
2. **Screen** — What's the underlying need? (food, companionship, medical, financial, safety)
3. **Match** — Identify 1–3 programs that fit; prioritize by urgency and access ease
4. **Explain plainly** — What it is, who qualifies, how to apply, what to expect
5. **Offer handoff** — Phone number, website, warm referral to AAA if needed
6. **Document** — Log referral in resident record (via MCP `log_interaction` tool)
7. **Follow up** — Caregiver dashboard flag to check in on referral completion
